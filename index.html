Strokes Gained Regression & Ranking Model

This interactive model demonstrates the adjusted ranking equation developed in my mathematics capstone. It uses four Strokes Gained categories to calculate a predicted round score. Adjust the category values to see how each one influences the result.

// ADJUSTED RANKING MODEL EQUATION

Predicted Score =

70.26386678 - 0.5(OTT) - 0.5(APP) - 0.5(ARG) - 0.25(PUTT)

The adjusted coefficients were selected to reduce the average ranking difference when compared with FedEx Cup standings.

Strokes Gained Inputs

+0.771

Performance on tee shots on par 4s and par 5s.

+0.626

+0.222

+0.495

Performance on shots taken on the putting surface.

Positive Strokes Gained values indicate performance above the benchmark. Because the model uses negative coefficients, higher Strokes Gained values reduce the predicted score.

Model Limitations

  • • Limited sample size.
  • • Regression dataset focused on the 2024 season, creating potential seasonal bias.
  • • Does not account for weather, course difficulty, or course length.
  • • Ranking comparison focused on the top ten rather than the entire PGA Tour standings.

Future Improvements

  • • Incorporate course difficulty and weather variables.
